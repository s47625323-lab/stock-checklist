A personal note for the M.Com Sem III batch

Hi,

I hope your semester is going well and the assignments aren't piling up too much!

I'm Ajit, and I wanted to share something personal with the M.Com Sem III batch. For a long time I've been quietly writing a novel, and it's finally out. It's called Ghar Wapsi: From Nothing to Everything, and it's now on Amazon.

I'm not selling anything big here. I just really wanted people like you to read it, because I wrote it with commerce students in mind.

The story

It's the story of Arjun, a 26-year-old CA firm manager in Delhi. He grew up moving from city to city and found comfort in the one thing that never changed: numbers. Then one phone call takes him back to his father's village, Devgaon.

🌼 Mustard fields 🔔 Temple bells 🏠 Dadi's house
💌 A girl who didn't wait 📒 Records that don't add up
Are numbers only meant to be correct, or are they meant to mean something?

Somewhere in there, Arjun has to figure that out. I think anyone who has spent hours on a balance sheet will feel close to him.
